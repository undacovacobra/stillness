Welcome. This meditation was made with Stillness, an app for creating your own guided sessions. You can try it free at the link below.

Let yourself sit somewhere comfortable. Notice what touches the floor, or the chair beneath you. Let the next breath be ordinary.

If a moment from work keeps replaying, quietly give it a name. A hard conversation. A task I didn’t finish. Then let your hands rest where they are.

Give your shoulders a little less to hold. Feel where the chair or floor is already supporting you. Breathe at whatever pace feels easy.

Think of one thing that can wait until tomorrow. Give it a place to go, like a note on a desk. There is room to come back to it later.

Come back to something you can feel right here. The weight of your feet. The warmth of your hands. Stay with one of those sensations for a few breaths.

There may still be things on your mind. As they come up, notice one more breath before you follow them. See what it is like to have this moment to yourself.

When you are ready, look around and notice one small detail in the room. Take that detail with you as you choose what comes next.
