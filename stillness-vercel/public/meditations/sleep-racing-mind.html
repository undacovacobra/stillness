Welcome. This meditation was made with Stillness, an app for creating your own guided sessions. You can try it free at the link below.

Let the bed take a little more of your weight. You can listen with your eyes open or closed.

Notice where the pillow meets your head. Feel the steady surface underneath your back. There is no particular way your breathing has to feel. Let the next breath come on its own.

A thought about tomorrow may come back. If you like, name it quietly. Planning. Rehearsing. Remembering. Then notice one place where your body is supported.

Let your tongue rest. Feel the space between your teeth. If your shoulders are holding anything, see what it is like to let them settle a little closer to the bed.

Picture setting the next thought beside you for the night, like a book you can open in the morning. There is room for it to be there while you rest here.

You might notice the weight of your hands, or the warmth under the covers. Choose one small sensation and stay with it for a few ordinary breaths.

When attention wanders, take your time coming back. Maybe it returns to your hands. Maybe it returns to the gentle sound in the room. Let that be enough to come back to.

Soften around your eyes, as much as feels comfortable. Let the muscles in your forehead take a break. Beneath all the movement of your thoughts, the bed is already holding you.

Allow the breath to fade into the background. There is less to follow now. The quiet can be a place to settle, one moment at a time.

If you are still awake, feel the spot where the covers touch you. It is a small, steady feeling. Rest your attention there for a while.

As the sounds grow quieter, let the space between my words widen. You can drift, or you can stay just as you are. Let the night take its own pace.

From here, there is nothing more to follow. Let your body be as heavy as it wants to be. Whenever you are ready, allow yourself to drift.
